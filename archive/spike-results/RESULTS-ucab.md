## Methodology

Two load disciplines over identical traces:

- **Closed-loop** (replay_bench.py / hub-demo `-trace`): one payment in
  flight, send → receipt (receipt-waited), next payment only after the
  previous completed. Measures per-tx latency; throughput is
  latency-bound, NOT the node's capacity.
- **Open-loop / paced** (paced_bench.py, new): every payment is dispatched
  AT its trace timestamp relative to run start, regardless of earlier
  payments' completion. Per-sender worker threads allocate nonces at
  dispatch time (strictly sequential per account); receipts are collected
  by a background poller (10 ms, per-sender head-of-queue — receipts land
  in nonce order per account). Measures whether the node keeps up with the
  workload's *native cadence*:
  - **lateness** = receipt_time − scheduled_time (scheduled = start + trace
    t_ms) — includes queueing behind the sender's earlier txs;
  - **miss rate** = share of payments with lateness > the tick budget
    (50 ms for the 20 Hz tank trace, 100 ms for the 10 Hz UC-A/UC-B);
  - **send lag** = send-completion − scheduled_time (dispatcher + signing +
    `eth_sendRawTransaction` + per-account queueing);
  - **max in-flight backlog** = max(sent − receipted) at any instant.

  Per-account nonce ordering is REAL protocol behavior: one slow tx
  head-of-line-blocks that account's later txs. The harness keeps it and
  reports it (see findings) instead of cheating with per-payment throwaway
  accounts.

Traces (generated by make_uc_traces.py from tank-arena/workloads — the
same "datasimulator" interface as UC-C):

- **UC-A platooning** `trace-uca-4v-10hz-60s.jsonl`: 4 vehicles, chain
  topology, 10 Hz beacons, 60 s → 2400 payments (4 per 100 ms tick),
  40 pay/s offered. Generator semantics: each beacon's data CONSUMER (the
  message receiver = predecessor; vehicle-1 for the leader's beacon) pays
  the data PROVIDER (the beacon sender) 1 unit. Payer accounts and their
  offered rates: vehicle-0 → 10/s, vehicle-1 → 20/s (pays both the leader
  and vehicle-2), vehicle-2 → 10/s. vehicle-3 only receives.
- **UC-B PMU** `trace-ucb-1s-10hz-60s.jsonl`: 1 sensor (FNET/FDR-class
  10 Hz), 60 s → 600 payments, 10 pay/s offered, single payer (consumer-0
  pays pmu-0 per reading, data-marketplace model).
- **UC-C game** `trace-uc-c-6p-20hz-30s.jsonl` (unchanged from RESULTS.md):
  379 payments, 6 players, 20 Hz, 30 s → 12.7 pay/s offered, spread over
  6 payer accounts (~2.1/s each).

Perun mapping (`trace-uca…-tank.jsonl`, `trace-ucb…-tank.jsonl`, headers
inside the files): all UC-A/UC-B payments are pairwise → "hit" events
(payer↔payee virtual channel); no pay-the-hub flows → no "fire" events.
Dense player ints over sorted entity names (UC-A: vehicle-i → i, `-clients
4`; UC-B: consumer-0 → 0, pmu-0 → 1, `-clients 2`). EVM amounts: 1 unit =
1 gwei (replay_bench convention); Perun amounts: 1 unit = 0.001 ETH
(hub-demo `traceUnit`). Perun replay is closed-loop only (a `-paced` mode
is being added separately).

## Results

(numbers below)
### Paced (open-loop) at native cadence — 2026-07-19, run-ucab-batch.sh + hub-demo

UC-A platooning (2400 payments, 40.1 tx/s offered, 100 ms budget):
- Perun (-clients 4): 0/2400 misses, lateness mean 1.34 ms max 3.1 ms; 40 pay/s
- zkSync: 0/2400 misses, lateness mean 41.7 ms p90 53.1 max 93.2; 40.0 tx/s; gas 133,094
- Arbitrum: 2400/2400 missed (>100 ms), lateness mean 82.9 s (diverging), achieved 8.0 tx/s
UC-B PMU (600 payments, 10 tx/s offered):
- Perun (-clients 2): 0/600 misses, lateness mean 1.14 ms; 10 pay/s
- zkSync: 0/600, lateness mean 19.5 ms max 29.5; 10.0 tx/s
- Arbitrum: 597/600 missed, lateness mean 44.9 s (diverging), achieved 4.0 tx/s
UC-C game (379 payments, 12.7 tx/s offered, 50 ms budget):
- Perun: 0/379 (see RESULTS-perun-phase2.md) | zkSync: 46/379=12.1%, mean 29.4 ms | Arbitrum: 362/379=95.5%, mean 210 ms

### Closed-loop (one in flight)
- UC-A: Perun 2303 pay/s (2400/2400) | zkSync 8.9 tx/s, 111.9 ms | Arbitrum 4.0 tx/s, 250.4 ms
- UC-B: Perun 2158 pay/s (600/600) | zkSync 8.9 tx/s, 112.0 ms | Arbitrum 4.0 tx/s, 249.8 ms

KEY FINDING: under in-order per-account nonces the nitro-testnode sequencer
accepts ~1 tx/account/block (~250 ms) → per-payer cap ≈ 4 tx/s regardless of
offered rate. zkSync and Perun sustain every offered rate; Perun lateness is
~30× smaller than zkSync's.
