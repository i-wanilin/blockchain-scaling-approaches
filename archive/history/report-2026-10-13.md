## 2. The setup as it stands

### Hardware (7 boards in use)

| Board | Model | Role in UC1 | Role in UC2 | Wi-Fi |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bath0` | Pi 4, 8 GB, heatsinks | the stack under test (one at a time) | the stack under test | 2.4 GHz |
| `bath1` | Pi 3B+ | **sensor** (sends the data, receives payments); time server | **player 0 + Perun hub**; time server | 5 GHz |
| `bath2` | Pi 3B+ | customer | player 1 | 5 GHz |
| `bath7` | Pi 3B | customer | player 2 | 2.4 GHz |
| `bath4`, `bath5`, `bath6` | Pi 3B | customers | players 3–5 | 2.4 GHz |
| `bath3` | Pi 3B | dead since 10-05, idle in every run | — | — |
| your PC (WSL) | — | drives everything over SSH, deploys contracts, collects and analyses; **never in the measured path** | same | — |

Known hardware facts the thesis states: one Pi 4 only (no spare); the Pi 3Bs run
undervolted at ≈ 600 MHz under load; `bath1` rebooted 5 times (cable), one run replaced.

### Software on the boards

- **`bath0`:** Docker with the pinned images (Arbitrum Nitro v3.9.6, geth, reth, postgres,
  redis), our own arm64 `zksync_server` build, Anvil. Chain **snapshots** made on the PC
  and restored before every run, so each run starts from a byte-identical chain.
  Configs copied to `deploy/stack-config/`.
- **`bath1`…`bath7`:** `deploy/cluster sync` copies `measure/`, the recorder, the game
  (`tank-arena/arena`, `tank-arena/net`) and the cross-compiled `perun-node`, and builds a
  Python venv. Every run checks that each board runs exactly the PC's code.
- **Nothing starts at boot**; every process of a run is started and stopped by
  `deploy/cluster`.

### Accounts (keys, not machines)

All from the public test seed (`measure/keys.py`): UC1 sensor 100, customers 101–105;
UC2 arena/hub 110, players 111–116, token deployer 119. Contracts are deployed from the PC.

### Safety rails every run passes

Code check → cooling gate (each board within 3 °C of its idle temperature) → stack up from
snapshot → clock gate (all boards < 5 ms to `bath1`) → pre-flight (right SD card in each
board, right Wi-Fi radio, one kernel, power save off, `bath0`/`bath1` not throttled, no
`netem`, disk, only the expected stack up). A failing check stops the series.

## 3. UC1 — IoT micropayments: what happens in one run

**Story:** a sensor (`bath1`) sells measurement data. Five customers receive the live
stream and pay for each time window they received.

**Data:** GESL recording 6063 (a real micro-PMU, 120 rows/s). 15 minutes are replayed.

**Windows:** 1 s, 100 ms, 10 ms → each customer pays 1, 10 or 100 times per second
(5, 50, 500 per second offered in total). Price 10¹² wei per window, paid in ETH.

| # | Step | Where | Timed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Code check, `down`, cooling gate | PC → all boards | no |
| 2 | Stack up: **Arbitrum/zkSync** restore the snapshot; **Perun** starts a fresh Anvil and deploys the Adjudicator + AssetHolder from the PC | `bath0` | no |
| 3 | Clock gate, pre-flight | all | no |
| 4 | Fund the sensor and customers with ETH on the L1 | PC → `bath0` | no |
| 5 | Start recorders (every 5 s: temperature, clock, throttling, CPU, Wi-Fi…) | all | — |
| 6 | **Open**: each customer's payment client prepares to pay. **Rollups:** bridge ETH from L1 to the rollup. **Perun:** open a direct channel to the sensor and deposit ETH | customers → `bath0` | **yes** |
| 7 | Sensor starts streaming the recording to every customer over its own TCP connection (≈ 68 KB/s each) | `bath1` → customers | — |
| 8 | Each customer cuts the stream into windows; at the end of each window it hands one payment to its local payment client | each customer | — |
| 9 | **Payment:** **rollups** — one ETH transfer to the sensor, sent pipelined (up to 64 in flight on Arbitrum, 20 on zkSync), confirmed by receipt. **Perun** — one signed channel update, co-signed by the sensor, no transaction | customer → `bath0` or → `bath1` | **yes** (latency = window received → payment confirmed) |
| 10 | After 15 min: 60 s **drain**; whatever is still unconfirmed is *unresolved* | customers | — |
| 11 | **Close**: **Perun** — finalize channel, `concludeFinal`, withdraw (timed to confirmed withdrawal). **Rollups** — withdrawal submitted | customers → `bath0` | **yes** |
| 12 | Collect everything into `results/pi/uc1/<run id>/`, write `manifest.json`, stack down | PC | — |
| 13 | Later on the PC: `measure.analyze_uc1` → `summary.json`; `measure.aggregate` → mean ± sd over 3 runs | PC | — |

## 4. UC2 — the tank game: what happens in one match

**Story:** six players play a 60-second tank match (20 ticks/s, seed 42), each on its own
Pi. There is **no game server**: every board computes the whole game itself (lockstep) and
only the players' inputs travel between the boards. Shots and hits are paid in **points**
(our own ERC-20, no value): a shot costs 1 point (to the "arena" account), a hit moves 5
points from victim to shooter. Each player starts with 10 000 points.

Every match offers exactly the same **1 070 payments** (813 shots, 257 hits, 17.8/s).

| # | Step | Where | Timed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Code check (incl. game hash and reference-log hash), `down`, cooling gate, stack up, clock gate, pre-flight — as UC1 | all | no |
| 2 | **Token setup.** **Rollups:** fund accounts on the L1, bridge 0.5 ETH to the deployer (119), deploy PointsToken **on the rollup**, `mintBatch` 10 000 to each player, check balances. **Perun:** deploy Adjudicator, ERC-20 asset holder and PointsToken on Anvil, mint 10 000 per player and 50 400 to the hub | PC → `bath0` | no |
| 3 | Start recorders; **Perun:** start the hub on `bath1` | all | — |
| 4 | **Open.** **Rollups:** each player bridges 1 ETH for fees. **Perun:** each player approves + deposits all its points into a ledger channel with the hub; then the 15 virtual channels between the players open off-chain through the hub | players → `bath0` / hub | **yes** |
| 5 | The game (`arena.node`) starts on all six boards at once; they connect in a full mesh | players | — |
| 6 | Every tick each board sends its tank's input to the other five, waits for theirs, steps the same simulation | players | tick lag measured |
| 7 | When the game produces a shot or hit, the **paying** board hands it to its local payment client and carries on (the game never waits) | each player | — |
| 8 | **Payment:** **rollups** — one ERC-20 `transfer` (shot → arena, hit → shooter). **Perun** — shot: update in the ledger channel to the hub; hit: update in the virtual channel to the shooter | players → `bath0` or peer | **yes** (latency = generated → confirmed) |
| 9 | After 1 200 ticks: 60 s drain | players | — |
| 10 | **Close.** **Perun:** virtual channels settle into the ledger channels, then each player settles on the L1 and withdraws; hub last. **Rollups:** withdrawal submitted | players → `bath0` | **yes** |
| 11 | Collect: points balances from the chain, every board's game log compared with the **reference log** (a mismatch fails the run) | PC | — |
| 12 | `measure.analyze_uc2` → `summary.json`; aggregates over 3 runs | PC | — |

## 5. Results

### UC1 final series (27 runs, 15 min each, mean ± sd, n = 3)

| Window | System | Latency p50 | Latency p99 | Goodput / offered (per s) | Succeeded | Open | Close |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 s | Perun | **40 ms** | 76 ms | 5.0 / 5 | 100 % | 1.3 s | 2.6 s |
| 1 s | Arbitrum | **92 ms** | 358 ms | 5.0 / 5 | 100 % | 1.6 s | 0.4 s |
| 1 s | zkSync | **68 s** (≥ 76 s) | 182 s | 4.1 / 5 | 86 % | 2.6 s | 23 s |
| 100 ms | Perun | **35 ms** | 78 ms | 50 / 50 | 100 % | 1.3 s | 2.5 s |
| 100 ms | Arbitrum | **244 ms** | 0.4 s (one run 56 s) | 50 / 50 | 100 % | 1.8 s | 0.2 s |
| 100 ms | zkSync | **323 s** (≥ 444 s) | 859 s | 4.7 / 50 | 10 % | 2.5 s | 35 s |
| 10 ms | Perun (saturated) | 252 s (≥ 327 s) | 357 s | **158** / 500 | 34 % | 1.3 s | 2.4 s |
| 10 ms | Arbitrum (saturated) | 102 s (≥ 125 s) | 169 s | **370** / 500 | 79 % | 1.9 s | 0.2 s |
| 10 ms | zkSync (saturated) | 369 s (≥ 498 s) | 942 s | **4.7** / 500 | 1 % | 2.7 s | 27 s |

"≥" = lower bound counting the payments that never succeeded too. The chain's own
confirmation time (submitted → confirmed, p50): Perun 30–36 ms, Arbitrum 0.09 / 0.24 /
0.73 s, zkSync ≈ 22–23 s.

**What it says:**
- **Perun** is fastest per payment (≈ 35–40 ms, no fee per payment) but one channel
  carries only ≈ 28–31 updates/s on a Pi 3B (≈ 40 on the Pi 3B+). It saturates at 10 ms.
- **Arbitrum** keeps up at 1 s and 100 ms; at 10 ms it reaches ≈ 370/s, limited by the
  Pi 3B payment clients (a lower bound for Arbitrum, `bath0` at 86 % CPU).
- **zkSync** on a Pi 4 tops out at **≈ 4.1–4.7 payments/s** at every window — the chain's
  own limit (CPU-bound, U9). It cannot serve UC1 even at the 1 s window.
- Reconciliation (payments vs. final balances) holds in all 27 runs.

### UC2 series (9 matches, mean ± sd, n = 3; "guests" = players 1–5)

| | Perun | Arbitrum | zkSync |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payments succeeded (of 1 070) | **1 070** | **1 070** | 947 ± 70 |
| Latency p50, guests, shot / hit | **39 / 39 ms** | **195 / 207 ms** | **26 / 21 s** |
| Latency p99, guests, shot / hit | 82 / 315 ms | 444 / 526 ms | 69 / 70 s |
| Payment late for the next shot (guests) | **0.2 %** | **61 %** | **99.6 %** |
| Goodput (payments/s) | 17.9 | 17.9 | 8.3 |
| Game disturbed? (match stretch) | no (1.0005) | no (1.0005) | no (1.0006) |
| Open per player | 14 s (incl. waiting for all channels) | 1.7 s | 2.5 s |
| Close per player | 7.1 s | 0.3 s | 19 s |
| Gas per shot | 0 (off-chain) | 34 398 | 85 549 |
| `bath0` CPU | 2 % | 20 % | 98 % |

**What it says:**
- All three systems see the **identical game** (every board's log hash = reference in all
  9 runs). The game itself is never slowed by the payments.
- **Perun** pays within ≈ 40 ms, before the player's next shot in 99.8 % of cases; cost is
  in Open/Close only. Its hit tail follows head-on channel collisions.
- **Arbitrum** pays all, but ≈ 200 ms (its 250 ms block rhythm) — slower than a burst
  (one bullet every 100 ms), so 61 % of shots are not yet paid when the next one fires.
- **zkSync** confirms in tens of seconds and loses 6–19 % to the drain deadline (some
  execute later; balances still explained). Its burst rate (8.3/s) is about twice its UC1
  rate — why is not established.
- The cost trade-off: **Perun pays to open and close, nothing per shot; the rollups pay
  nothing to open but gas for every shot.**

### Wi-Fi baseline (no chain running)

Round trip ≈ **10 ms** on every pair (the floor under every latency above), no loss.
5 GHz pair ≈ 75 Mbit/s; Pi 3Bs on 2.4 GHz only 1–13 Mbit/s with a Python sender.

### What the numbers are limited by (stated in the thesis)

- One Wi-Fi network in one room = best case; no NAT, no distance.
- Pi 3Bs undervolted → their absolute numbers are lower bounds; it only affects the
  comparison where the boards are the limit (UC1 at 10 ms).
- n = 3 per cell; latency of saturated runs is shaped by the queue, hence the lower-bound column.
- UC2 checks payments, not rules: a player could refuse to pay a hit (honest workload).
